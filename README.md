## 🚀 Vision

PRAX is not just another passive to-do list. It is a **Personal AI Execution Agent** designed specifically for students and early-career professionals who juggle multiple commitments—college, internships, DSA preparation, AI learning, projects, and personal routines. 

Instead of requiring you to meticulously plan every hour of your day, PRAX:
- **Understands** your priorities, available time, and actual progress.
- **Decides** what you should focus on next.
- **Adapts** when reality changes (e.g., if you miss a study session or a deadline is moved up).
- **Follows up** with you to ensure accountability.

The goal is to reduce your cognitive overhead, keeping you focused on *doing* the work rather than *planning* it, while ensuring you remain in control of all consequential decisions.

## 🎯 Core Capabilities

- **Personal Task Management**: Track deadlines, priorities, recurring tasks, and important dates using natural language.
- **Adaptive Planning**: PRAX compares your plan against actual progress. If you fall behind, it intelligently reschedules based on available time and priority, explaining *why* the changes were made.
- **Proactive Accountability**: Follows up on planned sessions. If you miss a task, it doesn't just leave it overdue—it asks why, learns your behavior, and helps you recover.
- **Focus Sessions**: Initiate timed focus blocks for deep work (e.g., "Start a 60-minute DSA focus session") and track your actual accomplishments.
- **Privacy by Design**: You own your data. PRAX operates with strict boundaries, requiring user approval for consequential actions (like publishing a generated LinkedIn draft).

## 🧠 Why PRAX?

Normal reminder apps just tell you: *"Do X at 8 PM."*

**PRAX reasons:** *"You planned DSA at 8 PM, but you skipped yesterday and your project deadline is approaching. You have 90 minutes available tonight. I recommend 45 minutes of project work followed by 45 minutes of DSA. I moved the remaining DSA session to tomorrow."*

## 🛠️ Project Philosophy

1. **User First**: Built to solve real problems, not just to showcase AI.
2. **Agent, Not Chatbot**: PRAX decides, uses tools, acts, observes, and adapts.
3. **Useful Intervention**: Optimizes for the *right* amount of interaction, avoiding notification fatigue.
4. **Explainability**: Every scheduling change or decision is explained simply and transparently.
